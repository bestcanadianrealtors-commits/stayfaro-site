Privacy policy

Effective 4 October 2026. Applies to the Faro app and to stayfaro.app.

Faro exists to hold you to your own calendar. To do that it needs to read your calendar and remember what you answer. This page says exactly what it collects, where it is kept, who else sees it, and how to make it go away.

Who runs Faro

Faro is built and operated by its developer in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. For anything in this policy, write to bestcanadianrealtors@gmail.com.

Faro has no advertising, no analytics trackers, and does not collect your location from the phone. Where you are comes only from the addresses on your calendar and the ones you type in.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored in your own account with Supabase, which hosts Faro's database and server functions and encrypts data at rest. Each person's data is walled off from every other person's by database access rules. The long-lived key that lets Faro read your calendar while the app is closed is kept on the server in a store the app itself cannot read.

Who else sees it

Faro shares data only with the services it needs to work, and only the piece each one needs:

  • Google Calendar — Faro reads your events, and creates, changes or deletes one when you ask, with the permission you grant when you sign in ("View and edit events on all your calendars").
  • Google Tasks — Faro reads your tasks, and adds, changes or deletes one when you ask ("Create, edit, organize, and delete all your tasks"). Faro asks for nothing else in your Google account.
  • Google Maps (Routes) — the origin and destination addresses of a trip, to get a live drive time.
  • Expo and Apple push services — your push token and the text of each reminder, to deliver it to your phone.
  • Your browser's notification service (Google, Mozilla, Microsoft or Apple, depending on the browser) — each reminder, encrypted so that only your browser can read it, and only if you turn browser notifications on.
  • Waze — a destination address, only when you tap "Open in Waze".

Faro does not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train anything.

Google user data

Faro's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Calendar and task data is used only to provide the reminders, confirmations, travel checks and lists you see in the app, and to carry out the changes you ask for. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never read by a person except with your permission to fix a problem you report.

Your choices

  • Stop calendar access at any time from your Google account permissions. Faro's reminders stop with it.
  • Turn off reminders from your phone's notification settings, or use Silent mode in Faro's settings.
  • Remove a statement and every transaction from it, from the Money tab.
  • Delete your account and all of its data from inside Faro: Settings → Account → Delete account. Everything is erased immediately, including the stored calendar key, and Faro's access is removed from your Google account. You can also ask by emailing bestcanadianrealtors@gmail.com from the address you signed in with.

How long it is kept

For as long as you have an account. Reminders that have already been sent are cleared from the server after 14 days. Everything else is deleted when your account is.

Children

Faro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes and the change is described here.
