A steady light for the next 810 days.

Faro is a personal accountability app that runs on one date: your Day 1. It reads your calendar, speaks up before and after every event, asks whether you finished, and keeps a streak for each area of your life. Two misses reset a streak. Three partials reset it. The boring days become proof.

What it does

Five pillars, one quiet sixth

  • Live calendar Reads your Google Calendar, read-only. Knows where you have to be, and with live traffic, when to leave.
  • Voice reminders Speaks up before an event starts, before it ends, and asks Did you finish? three minutes after. It works with the app closed.
  • Streaks per goal Seven areas of life, one streak each. A Yes adds one. Two misses in a row reset it; three partials reset it.
  • Chapter clock Day X of 90, 270, or 810 on every screen, and the days until the next miracle moment.
  • Money mirror Upload a bank statement and see income against spending across the same seven areas, so money sits next to time.
  • The sleep contract A bedtime and a wake-up alarm above everything else, each with its own streak.

Your data

Built to be trusted with your calendar

Faro reads your calendar and never writes to it. Your events, goals, streaks and statements stay in your own account. There are no ads, no trackers, and nothing is sold. The full privacy policy says exactly what is collected, where it is stored, and how to delete it.

Faro's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
